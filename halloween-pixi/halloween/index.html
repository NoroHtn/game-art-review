How to play

Match 3 or more paying symbols from the left on any of 25 fixed paylines. WILD substitutes for paying symbols on reels 2–5.

Land 6 or more coins to enter Hold & Win. Coins lock in place. You start with 3 respins; every new coin resets the counter to 3. Fill all 15 cells for the 3,000× Grand award.

The cauldron meters show the existing demo’s collection progress. Their additional feature rewards are not implemented in the original demo.

Buy Bonus costs 70× your current bet. All play uses demo credits. This is unvalidated demo mathematics; no RTP or real-money readiness is claimed.
