Crash Game layout contract

Review specification — September 2026

Confirmed composition

Keep the multiplier centered horizontally and vertically within the gameplay viewport. The sidebar, history strip, header and bet dock do not contribute to that center. The five-second setup starts in the bottom-left. Once the grip is secured, the connected alien/UFO, cow and farmer group moves toward the top-right. Approved artwork is used in timed poses; a continuous character rig remains production work.

Preserve the muted vintage rubber-hose art direction from the earlier references. None of the earlier illustrations is the approved layout. Approved model boards and the launch storyboard establish the cast and grip. Separated rig layers, continuous animation and final contact cleanup remain production work.

Desktop and mobile layout

Desktop uses a persistent left results panel with Live Bets, History and Top Wins tabs. Gameplay occupies the right column; two betting panels sit side by side below it. In single-bet mode, the remaining card expands and a separate + control restores the second card.

Mobile uses one column: game header, recent multipliers, gameplay, two stacked bet cards, then the results frame. Removing Bet B closes the gap and raises the results frame. A + Add bet control restores B. Both active cashout buttons fit in the standalone 844-pixel-high viewport at 360, 390 and 430 CSS pixels wide. The full results table scrolls below the betting controls. Operator/browser chrome reduces available height and still requires integration review; do not treat the standalone checks as proof for every embedded viewport.

The HTML responds to viewport width. PNGs show desktop 1440 × 900 and mobile full-content captures at width 390. Mobile images are intentionally taller than one viewport to show the results list. SVG exports preserve editable text and geometry, with simplified background decoration. The final Figma exports define the visual target. The HTML demonstrates the proposed interaction behavior.

Per-panel controls

  • Amount frame with editable decimal value and decrement/increment buttons.
  • Quick amounts 1, 10, 25 and 50, matching the references.
  • Auto cashout checkbox and numeric multiplier target, with a visible × suffix.
  • Main action showing state and stake or estimated gross return.
  • Auto Bet setup with a finite 1–10-round demo count and an explicit stop action.
  • Persistent receipt/error line independent of the main action.
  • Remove control on the second panel; + restore control in single-bet mode.

Demo values use an amount range of 0.05–1,000.00, an increment/decrement of 1.00 clamped at the minimum, and auto targets of 1.01×–100.00×. These are prototype assumptions, not approved production limits. Quick amounts replace the amount rather than adding to it. Stake and target editing are disabled while that bet is pending, accepted, live or queued.

Bet B cannot be removed while it has an unresolved or queued wager. Cancel it while cancellation is permitted, or wait for its result. Removing a settled/idle panel stops future Auto Bet on that panel and never hides outstanding exposure.

Cashout and demo behavior

Cashout creates a small nonblocking collected-amount pop next to the corresponding button. The receipt remains after the pop fades. The shared scene and other bet continue. A confirmed/accepted cashout is not reversed by a later crash. In this local demo, a cashout request is treated as accepted when invoked; a 550 ms acknowledgement delay demonstrates the pending UI. This is not a simulation of production server arbitration, rejected requests or delayed wallet settlement.

Manual toolbar actions advance the shared round: start, increase multiplier by 0.25×, end, then open the next betting window. Auto cashout settles at its configured target when a demo step crosses it. Auto Bet applies the panel's stake for a finite number of rounds and stops when explicitly stopped, on insufficient funds, disconnection or panel removal. This prototype uses no real wagers. Started rounds use a local random crash value between 1.00× and 5.00× for demonstration; this is not production game mathematics.

Example ledger: 1,000.00 starting balance minus two 10.00 accepted stakes leaves 980.00. One accepted cashout at 2.48× credits 24.80, leaving 1,004.80 while the other bet remains live. If the other bet loses, round net is +4.80. Both bets collected at 2.48× would leave 1,029.60.

Results frame

Live Bets displays labeled sample player IDs, stake, collected multiplier and gross return. Its sample totals reconcile to the shown rows. History records local demo settlements by round and position, including losing outcomes. Top Wins shows fixed sample results for today ordered by gross return. These are not claimed live network data or production telemetry. Recent round multiplier history remains above gameplay, distinct from personal bet History.

Production validation checklist

Before production sign-off, verify amount controls, bet acceptance, independent cashouts, queued-bet cancellation, second-panel removal, history and results. Check both cashout actions at 360, 390 and 430 px phone widths, including operator and browser chrome. Repeat these checks against the integrated runtime and supported browsers.
