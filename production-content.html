05 / PRODUCTION

A clear path from concept to handoff.

A proposed 15-working-day art cycle with parallel UI, illustration and animation work. Reviews happen at four gates, before dependent work moves forward.

Team and workload

Planning estimates, not recorded hours. One UI Designer, one 2D Artist and one Spine Animator, with part-time Art Lead support. The cycle excludes backend, game mathematics, audio and certification.

Handoff acceptance

Named source assets and exports; documented dimensions and pivots; a separated layer inventory; the complete control-state library; motion timings and reduced-motion behavior; a Figma presentation and matching PDF with working access.

The current website demonstrates the visual direction and interactions. A finished Spine rig and final Figma/PDF submission are separate deliverables.
