03 / DRAWING SYSTEM

Rules for a consistent art pass

01

Contour hierarchy

A strong outer silhouette, lighter interior construction and selective fine detail. Keep line weight coherent across the entire cast.

02

Shape before texture

Use broad muzzle, body and limb shapes. Make the pose readable in a flat silhouette before adding surface treatment.

03

Connected anatomy

Track near and far limbs. Keep jaw, neck, legs and hooves attached convincingly through the lift pose. Avoid fused fingers at the farmer's grip.

04

Restrained grain

Use light paper texture on broad painted areas. Preserve crisp faces, grip edges and silhouettes; keep grain out of UI text and numbers.

05

Simple volume

Create depth with overlapping forms and a few painted shadow planes. Preserve the illustrated, matte cartoon finish.

06

Actual-size readability

Review every asset in both finished layouts. Simplify small features when needed; preserve the lower-left launch origin and clear diagonal flight path.

04 / CHARACTER DIRECTION

One connected performance

These rules guide the approved cast and its animation handoff. The final model boards are available in the character chapter.

COW / MAIN CHARACTER

Goofy and uncomprehending

A broad soft muzzle, floppy ears and a slightly bewildered expression. The body remains readable beneath the patches; all four legs have a clear construction.

First check

Can we read the expression and count or account for every limb at mobile size?

FARMER / COMIC COUNTERPOINT

Determined, visibly outmatched

A readable face and hat, two distinct arms and a stable grip on the cow. The hanging pose expresses effort while preserving a clean connected silhouette.

First check

Are both hands understandable, and does the same contact point survive the lift poses?

ALIEN + UFO / SUPPORTING CAUSE

Small, clear and secondary

The saucer and beam explain the lift immediately. The alien stays readable inside the dome, with fewer details than the cow and farmer.

First check

Does the group read as abduction without enlarging it into the multiplier area?

Motion follows the shared round

Use bounded lift and sway while the round is live. Personal cashout produces its small local amount pop beside that bet button. The farmer releases and the cow is abducted only at the shared crash event; the animation never predicts the hidden outcome.

SLIDE 01 / IDEA AND THEME

MOO-NAPPED!

A vintage farm-comedy crash game. A UFO lifts a confused cow while a farmer hangs on. Players place a bet and cash out as the multiplier rises, before the farmer loses his grip and the cow is abducted.

Two independent bet positions share the same round. Personal cashout confirms that player's return while the shared scene continues.

SLIDE 02 / TARGET AUDIENCE

Familiar decisions, distinctive character performance

The proposed audience is adults of legal gambling age who play short-session instant games, including players familiar with crash mechanics. This is a design hypothesis, not validated audience research.

The interface prioritizes the live multiplier, clear amounts and directly accessible cashout actions. The vintage comedy theme gives the experience a recognizable visual identity without adding another gameplay rule.

SLIDE 03 / VISUAL PRINCIPLES

Readable decisions and expressive staging

The multiplier stays at the center of gameplay. A compact cow, farmer and UFO group begins in the bottom-left and travels toward the top-right after the five-second setup. Bold silhouettes, muted farm colors and restrained texture preserve clarity on desktop and mobile.

The intended distinction from the supplied aircraft and rocket references is character-based slapstick: the cow's confusion and the farmer's effort provide the round's visual story. Any effect on recall or engagement remains to be tested.
