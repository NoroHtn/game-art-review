01 / CONCEPT & BRIEF

A little farm. An unexpected lift.

This character-led crash concept is built around a cow, an alien and a farmer who refuses to let go.

01

Idea & theme

A quiet moonlit farm becomes a slapstick abduction. The UFO lifts the cow; the farmer grabs her rear leg. Their connected silhouette turns the rising round tension into a visual story.

02

Core decision

Place a bet, watch the multiplier rise, and collect before the shared round ends. Two independent positions share one round. A personal cashout settles only that bet and never interrupts the scene.

03

Intended audience

Adults of legal gambling age who are familiar with short-session crash and instant games. This is an audience hypothesis, not a validated research finding. The website is an art prototype with no real wagers.

04

Why it stands out

Farmyard comedy replaces an abstract graph, aircraft or rocket. The cow’s bewilderment and the farmer’s effort create a recognizable cast without adding another gameplay rule. Any effect on player recall remains to be tested.

Visual principles

Read the decision first

The multiplier is the primary scene information. Amounts, cashout actions and receipts stay legible and separate from the illustration.

Silhouette before texture

Large ink shapes, expressive faces and clear extremities carry the comedy. Texture supports the print character without obscuring the grip or UI.

One connected performance

UFO, beam, cow and farmer respond to the same pulling force. The five-second setup stays at bottom-left before flight moves toward top-right.

Assignment scope

The required presentation demonstrates concept, art direction, a moodboard, a main game screen and production leadership. The interactive website adds inspectable supporting evidence; it does not replace the required Figma presentation and PDF.
